# Media — opening skit + in-world ads

Stills generated with Grok Imagine from the in-game AUS101 look (copper/gold tube T-101, red eyes, SPF bottle) and a Gold Coast bunker.

## Cutscene (`cutscene/`)

Played after the poster tap (`src/poster/CutsceneReel.js`). Tap skips.

1. `s1_bunker_bay.jpg` — secret Gold Coast bunker, operatives, time-cargo
2. `s2_captured_units.jpg` — captured copper T-101s in cages
3. `s3_what_now.jpg` — operatives stuck on what to do with them
4. `s4_i_can_use_them.jpg` — health-department officer claims the units for a skin-cancer PSA
5. `s5_psa_apply.jpg` — punchline: robot applying zinc on the beach

`aus101_ref.jpg` is the hero reference for later ads.

## Ads (`ads/`)

- `billboard_terminate_uv.png` — in-world boardwalk billboard (lettering added in PIL)

## Apply mode

Hold Space next to a Ken or babe: zinc coverage tints their skin paler/shinier. Kids, gulls, goths, and SIGMA_07 are not paint targets.
